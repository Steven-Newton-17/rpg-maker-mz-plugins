# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Lights: a `<glow>` or `<light:radius,colour,offset>` event lights a flickering pool of ground round it, and characters in it are lit too.
- Parameters for the default light radius, colour and strength.

## 1.0.0

- Characters on night maps are darkened to the night tilesets' tone.
- Events marked `<glow>` (fires, lamps) stay at full brightness.
- Per-map `<night>`, `<night:r,g,b>` and `<day>` notes; script calls to darken or clear a map.
